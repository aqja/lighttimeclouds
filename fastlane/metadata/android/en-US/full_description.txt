<p>LightTimeClouds is a home screen widget: an ordinary analog clock face with a ring around it that shows the course of the day at your place, from night through dawn and daylight to dusk, plus the moon with its current phase. Inspired by sunclock.net and the weather clock OpenSky, with a <b>24-hour dial</b> by default (12 hours optional, or noon at the top).</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Sky ring:</b> each section of the ring shows the sky from the horizon upward at that time of day. Daylight, sunrise and sunset, the blue hour and the night come from a physical light scattering model, as the human eye sees the sky. Sun and moon stand in it at their real height, optionally with their paths over the day.</li>
<li><b>Moon:</b> with its phase, as a hand of its own or on the hour hand; its distance from the center shows the moon's altitude, with the moon's daily path.</li>
<li><b>Optional weather ring</b> (forecast by Open-Meteo): cloud cover as grey, precipitation in blue (snow violet), darker with the amount and more opaque with the probability. Air or apparent temperature as a scale around the rim or between the numerals, and the weather in words beside or below the clock ("A bit cooler than this time yesterday. Light rain likely from this evening.").</li>
<li>Marks for solar noon and midnight, sunrise and sunset, and optionally the next alarm.</li>
<li>Saved places with their own time zone: several widgets make a world clock. Places can be shared from Organic Maps, OsmAnd and other map apps.</li>
<li>"Times" tab in the app with all sun events of the day, moonrise and moonset, the moon phase, which opens to the dates of the coming cycle, and the coming moon events: supermoon and micromoon, lunar and solar eclipses visible at the place, and the lunar standstills.</li>
<li>"Weather" tab with the forecast drawn into the sky of every hour: clouds, wind, rain and snow, air and apparent temperature, 24 hours at a time to swipe through, and the details of each hour at a tap.</li>
<li>Light, dark or automatic color mode, numerals and hands in your Material You colors. Freely resizable, several widgets with their own settings.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy:</b> no tracking, no ads, no location permission (places are entered by hand or shared from a map app). The app only goes online for the optional weather ring: about every two hours it sends the coordinates of your places, rounded to about 100 m, to open-meteo.com to fetch the forecast.</p>

<p>Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Sun and moon from the theories ELP 2000-82B and VSOP87 (IMCCE, Paris Observatory), sky colors ported from Horizon (MIT).</p>
